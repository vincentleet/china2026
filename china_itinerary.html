  • Optional Plank Walk from South Peak: a narrow wooden ledge bolted to a sheer cliff face — harness hire ~¥30, not required, entirely at the group's comfort level
  • Walk back South Peak → West Peak (~40 min), then cable car down — back at Huashan North by around noon
  • Return train to Xi'an — back in the city by ~1:30–2pm, leaving the afternoon fully free
☀️ Afternoon

🌙 Evening
  • Dinner in the Muslim Quarter: lamb skewers, persimmon cakes, biangbiang noodles
