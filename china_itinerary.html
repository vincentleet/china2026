Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City & Jingshan Park

Beijing 🏨 Chongwenmen Hotel, Beijing
🌅 Morning
  • Early start — Tiananmen Square at opening for photos before crowds build

Day 5·Sat 17 Oct

Temple of Heaven, Zhongguancun, 798 Art District & escape room

Beijing 🏨 Chongwenmen Hotel, Beijing
🌅 Morning
  • Very early start — Temple of Heaven park at 7am: locals doing tai chi, flying kites, playing erhu, completely unscripted
  • The light on the Hall of Prayer at that hour is one of the best photography opportunities in Asia — Vincent, Jeremy, and Dad bring cameras
  • 🍜 Hand-Pulled Noodles class at The Hutong — 10:30–13:00, teacher Sophia Du (杜仁娟). Learn to pull Lamian noodles from scratch and prepare a Xinjiang sauce. Small-group, English-language, finishing with lunch. Venue is in the hutong area; bring the address/map, or call if lost: +86 159 0104 6127. Not yet booked — add to checklist
☀️ Afternoon
  • Zhongguancun Electronics District (Haidian) — Beijing's version of Akihabara, spread across several interconnected malls; phone repair shops (good for iPhone battery replacement — ¥120–200 for a genuine battery, same day), keyboard and peripheral stalls, retro handhelds (Anbernic, Powkiddy), and Chinese-market tech that doesn't reach NZ. An hour here is more than enough unless you want to go deep
  • 798 Art District for Cold War-era factory architecture, outdoor sculpture, and street art — great for photography and design interest; 15-minute taxi from Zhongguancun
  • 🎴 Pokémon TCG Gym, Xidan Joy City — B1 floor, Xidan Joy City mall: China's licensed equivalent of a Pokémon Center, with dedicated play areas, regional decor, and a huge inventory of Simplified Chinese TCG cards and exclusives Vincent
🌙 Evening
  • Group escape room in Sanlitun — book an English-compatible room well in advance, this is a group non-negotiable
  • Optional late night: Guijie Ghost Street hotpot — restaurants open until 3am, zero tourists

Day 9·Wed 21 Oct

Mount Huashan — West Peak cable car & South Peak

Xi'an 🏨 AHS-Park Hotel, Xi'an
🌅 Morning
  • Train from Xi'an North → Huashan North station (~35 min, ~¥35–50 pp) — aim to depart around 7:30am
  • Shuttle from station to West cable car base (~20 min, ~¥30 pp)
  • West Peak cable car up to West Peak (2,082m) — stunning enclosed gondola ride (~20 min, ¥280 pp round-trip)
  • Park entrance fee ¥160 pp — mandatory, covers the whole mountain
☀️ Afternoon
  • Walk West Peak → South Peak (~600m, ~30 min) — the summit at 2,154m is the highest point on the mountain with panoramic views across the Shaanxi plains
  • Optional Plank Walk from South Peak: a narrow wooden ledge bolted to a sheer cliff face — harness hire ~¥30, not required, entirely at the group's comfort level
  • Walk back South Peak → West Peak (~40 min), then cable car down — back at Huashan North by around noon
  • Return train to Xi'an — back in the city by ~1:30–2pm, leaving the afternoon fully free
☀️ Afternoon (continued)
  • Back in Xi'an by ~1:30–2pm — quick rest and lunch at the hotel or nearby
  • ~3:00pm: City Wall bike ride — hire bikes at the South or North gate (¥45 pp for 100 min) and ride the full 14km loop atop the Ming-dynasty walls; the afternoon light in October turns the ancient stone a warm amber, and the city views in both directions are superb
🌙 Evening
  • Dinner in the Muslim Quarter: lamb skewers, persimmon cakes, biangbiang noodles
Mount Huashan West cable car (¥280 pp) West Peak → South Peak South Peak 2,154m Plank Walk (optional, ¥30) ~3pm City Wall bike ride (¥45 pp) 📌 ~¥535–560 pp total · half-day on mountain 📌 Park entry ¥160 mandatory

  • Allow 2–3 hours total in the park; the gardens are stunning at night with full illuminations
  • Final Xi'an dinner before or after in the Muslim Quarter — spiced lamb skewers, rose jelly (méiguī bīng fěn), persimmon cakes

Day 12·Sat 24 Oct

Bund at dawn, French Concession, M50 & teamLab Borderless

Shanghai 🏨 JI Hotel The Bund, Shanghai
🌅 Morning
  • Very early: Bund at 6:30am — almost tourist-free, golden light on the art deco facades, Pudong skyline reflected in still water
  • Best photography window of the entire Shanghai leg — Vincent, Jeremy, and Dad bring cameras
  • Mid-morning: French Concession wander into Xintiandi — start on the tree-lined Art Deco streets of the Concession, then walk south into Xintiandi's two blocks of restored 1920s shikumen lane houses: upscale cafés, independent boutiques, good lunch options. The architecture is intact at street level in a way you won't see anywhere else in Shanghai. 30 minutes of wandering is plenty; have lunch here before splitting into groups for the afternoon
☀️ Afternoon
  • Tianzifang alleyways for Sharon and Mum — independent boutiques, textile shops, handmade jewellery (a short metro or taxi from Xintiandi)
  • M50 Moganshan Road art district for Vincent and Sharon — 100+ open studios, ceramics workshops, galleries in converted factories
  • 🎴 Pokémon Center Card Gym — B1, Wujiaochang Wanda Plaza: official Simplified Chinese TCG cards, battle areas, exclusive collectibles and themed plushies (open 10:00–21:00) Vincent

Day 13·Sun 25 Oct

Nanjing day trip — Ming tombs, city walls & Qinhuai River at night

Shanghai 🏨 JI Hotel The Bund, Shanghai
🌅 Morning
  • High-speed train from Shanghai Hongqiao → Nanjing South (~1h15m, ~¥100–130 pp) — depart by 8am
  • Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (Zhongshan Ling) — 392 steps through a forested hillside to a spectacular hilltop tomb; crowds are lighter in the morning
  • Ming Xiaoling Mausoleum nearby — Sacred Way lined with stone animal statues, one of China's best-preserved imperial burial sites
☀️ Afternoon
  • Nanjing City Wall — the longest surviving ancient city wall in the world (35km); take the wall walk near Zhonghua Gate for the best section
  • Zhonghua Gate fortress — six-century-old gate castle with hidden tunnels and soldier vaults cut into the walls, a highlight for Tom and Dad
  • Late afternoon: wander into the old town lanes south of the gate to rest before the evening
🌙 Evening
  • 🏮 Qinhuai River Scenic Area (Confucius Temple) — the entire riverfront lights up at dusk with red lanterns, ornate bridges and reflected neon on the water; best views from the boats and the bridge walkways (open until 10pm) Vincent
  • Dinner in the Confucius Temple food street — try Nanjing duck (the city's signature dish), small wontons and osmanthus rice cakes
  • Train back to Shanghai — last trains around 9:30–10pm, back at hotel by midnight

Day 15·Tue 27 Oct

Split morning, hairy crab lunch & last evening in Shanghai

Shanghai 🏨 JI Hotel The Bund — last night
🌅 Morning
  • Yu Garden & Old Town (豫园) — a 16th-century Ming dynasty garden in the heart of the old city; ¥40 pp; ornamental rockwork, dragon-topped pavilion walls, koi ponds, and covered walkways. Go early (open 8:30am) before the afternoon tour groups arrive. The surrounding Yuyuan Bazaar is touristy but the food stalls are excellent — xiaolongbao, pan-fried pork buns, and osmanthus cake. A 10-minute walk to/from the hotel
  • 🎴 JE宝可梦卡牌 — Room 212, Baimi Xiangxie, Guangxi North Road, Huangpu (near Nanjing East Road metro): dedicated Japanese PTCG singles dealer, the best source for individual Japanese cards in Shanghai; Wednesday night tournaments at 19:30. Worth visiting in the morning while the group is in the Yu Garden / Old Town area — same neighbourhood, 10-minute walk Vincent
  • After Yu Garden: split by interest — Sharon and Tom: wet market or xiaolongbao cooking class via UnTour Food Tours; Vincent and Jeremy: electronics markets (see notes below); Mum and Sharon: AP Xinyang Fashion Market or South Bund Fabric Market for bargain shopping
☀️ Afternoon
  • Hairy crab lunch — late October is peak hairy crab season in Shanghai, one of China's great seasonal food events; book a local restaurant in advance
  • Electronics markets for Vincent and Jeremy: Baisihao Electronics City, Xujiahui (Metro Lines 1/9/11, Exit 1) — stalls selling Anbernic/Powkiddy handhelds, keyboards, switches, keycaps, and Onyx Boox e-readers at factory-adjacent prices; AP Xinyang Market, Science Museum station (same Metro stop as the science museum) for tech accessories and components
  • Shanghai Science and Technology Museum (上海科技馆) — Metro Line 2, SSTM Station (Exit 7/8); ¥45 adults; open Tue–Sun 9:00–17:15; multiple floors covering robotics, space, earth sciences, life sciences, and three IMAX theatres; particularly strong for Tom; if the Binjiang Park view didn't happen on Day 11, this is in the same Pudong neighbourhood — easy to combine
🌙 Evening
  • Final Shanghai dinner — last night in China, so make it count; the Bund-facing restaurants in the JI Hotel neighbourhood, or book ahead for a rooftop view across the river
  • Post-dinner walk along the Bund if the weather holds — it reads completely differently at night, all lit up
