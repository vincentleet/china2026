Day 3
Thu 15 Oct
Beijing

Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City & Jingshan Park

  • Early start — Tiananmen Square at opening for photos before crowds build
  • Full morning inside the Forbidden City — the scale genuinely needs time, don't rush it
  • Tickets must be booked online weeks in advance (daily cap, no walk-ups at the gate)
  • After lunch: Jingshan Park directly north — the hilltop pavilion gives the single best overhead view of the Forbidden City's golden rooftops in Beijing
  • Almost no Western tourists here despite being extraordinary — Dad, Jeremy, and Vincent bring cameras
  • Evening: Peking duck dinner near Wangfujing — book a well-regarded local restaurant, not a tourist trap
Tiananmen Square Forbidden City Jingshan Park views Peking duck dinner 📌 Pre-book Forbidden City tickets
🏨Sleep: Beijing
Day 4
Fri 16 Oct
Beijing

Great Wall at Mutianyu — full day

  • Private van for the group — much better than tour buses, depart hotel ~8am
  • Mutianyu is the right section: fewer crowds than Badaling, well-restored, beautiful valley setting
  • Cable car up to the wall — saves legs for the ridge walk between watchtowers
  • Mid-October foliage in the surrounding forest is at or near peak — the colours from the wall are extraordinary
  • Optional toboggan run back down — fast, fun, good for the group
  • Walking the full ridge between towers is a legitimate hike with real elevation changes
  • Back in Beijing by late afternoon — rest evening or nearby dinner
Mutianyu Great Wall Cable car up Autumn foliage Ridge walk Toboggan (optional) 📌 Book private van for 6
🏨Sleep: Beijing
Day 5
Sat 17 Oct
Beijing

Temple of Heaven (dawn), Fragrant Hills, 798 Art District & escape room

  • Very early start — Temple of Heaven park at 7am: locals doing tai chi, flying kites, playing erhu, completely unscripted
  • The light on the Hall of Prayer at that hour is one of the best photography opportunities in Asia — Vincent, Jeremy, and Dad bring cameras
  • Then Fragrant Hills for peak autumn foliage — smoke trees turn brilliant red-orange in mid-October, best on a weekday to avoid crowds
  • Late afternoon: 798 Art District for Cold War-era factory architecture, outdoor sculpture, and street art — great for photography and design interest
  • Evening: group escape room in Sanlitun — book an English-compatible room well in advance, this is a group non-negotiable
  • Optional late night: Guijie Ghost Street hotpot if the group has energy — restaurants open until 3am, zero tourists
Temple of Heaven dawn Fragrant Hills foliage 798 Art District Escape room evening Ghost Street hotpot (optional) 📌 Book escape room ahead — English room

Xi'an — 2 full days (Days 8–9)
Day 8
Tue 20 Oct
Xi'an

Terracotta Warriors — full day

  • Full day at the Mausoleum of Qin Shi Huang — the most historically significant day of the entire trip
  • Visit all three pits plus the bronze chariot museum — don't skip Pit 2 and 3, they're less crowded and equally fascinating
  • Book a private English-speaking guide in advance — context transforms this from impressive to extraordinary
  • Allow the full day; it earns it — most people wish they'd spent longer
  • Early evening: Shaanxi History Museum if energy allows — free entry, advance online booking required, Tang dynasty gold treasures section is extraordinary for Tom and Dad
  • Dinner: fresh biang biang noodles at a local canteen — watch the cook hand-pull the noodles from a single lump of dough
Terracotta Warriors All three pits Bronze chariot museum Private English guide Shaanxi Museum (eve) 📌 Pre-book tickets & guide 📌 Shaanxi Museum — free, book online

Day 9
Wed 21 Oct
Xi'an

Mount Huashan — full day hike

  • Very early start — train from Xi'an North to Huashan North station (~35 min) then shuttle to cable car base
  • Cable car up to North Peak — saves legs for the ridge hike between peaks
  • Hike East → West → South peaks if the group has the legs — chains assist on the steep sections
  • South Peak at 2,154m offers 360° views across the Shaanxi plains — breathtaking in mid-October clear air
  • Optional Plank Walk: a narrow ledge bolted to a sheer vertical cliff face — not for the faint-hearted, entirely optional
  • Back in Xi'an by early evening — City Wall at golden hour if any energy remains (the low October light turns the ancient stone warm amber)

Day 12
Sat 24 Oct
Shanghai

Hangzhou day trip — West Lake & Longjing tea village

  • Early train from Shanghai Hongqiao station (~45 min) — depart by 7:30am to get ahead of day-trippers
  • Boat ride on West Lake — one of China's most celebrated landscapes, especially in mid-October foliage
  • Walk the ancient Su Causeway — tree-lined, willow-draped, gorgeous in autumn
  • Leifeng Pagoda for panoramic lake views — escalators to the ruins, stairs to the top
  • After lunch: taxi to Longjing tea village — watch hand-roasting of Dragon Well green tea and taste fresh from the farm
  • Train back to Shanghai by 6pm — dinner near the hotel, early night

Shanghai Disneyland — full day

  • Arrive at park opening — head straight to Zootopia: Hot Pursuit (queues hit 90–120 min by mid-morning, the world's only Zootopia land)
  • TRON Lightcycle Power Run next — exclusive to Shanghai, you lean forward on a lightcycle and race through a neon digital world at speed

  • Fully bilingual park — all signage, ride instructions, and cast members operate in English
